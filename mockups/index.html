Design system

Every token lives in assets/tokens.css and nowhere else — colour, type, radius and spacing. Pages reference variables; no page hardcodes a colour. Both themes are drawn deliberately: dark is its own set of values, not an inverted light.

Platform colours identify a brand next to its name — a dot or a chip. They never carry a value on their own: Meta blue and LinkedIn blue are too close to tell apart as chart fills, and TikTok's black has no hue to read. Charts use the validated series colours below instead.

Chart series

series-1
series-2

Both steps clear the lightness band, chroma floor, colour-vision-deficiency separation, normal-vision floor and 3:1 contrast against the chart surface, in light and dark. Charts also keep a legend and a data table, so identity never rests on colour alone.
